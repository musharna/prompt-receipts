Vintage Camper Van - 6 studs wide, 12 long

Happy birthday! Each step is a top-down map; the top edge is the front of the van. Use any brick sizes that fit the colored squares (bigger bricks = faster, and more solid). Steps 2-5 are bricks, steps 1, 6 and 7 are flat plates.

Step 1: Base plate

Lay a black 6x12 plate flat (front of the van = top of the map). Turn it over and press two 2x4 axle plates (each with two wheels) on the underside, centred on rows 2-3 and rows 9-10. Flip it back: the wheels should stick out of both sides.

New studs this step: 72 Black

Step 2: Lower body, first brick layer

Build the walls of the van. Start with the light-gray bumpers across the front and back rows, then add teal. Leave the middle empty (the floor stays visible) and leave the four wheel-arch gaps open. Row 4 is a solid teal wall: it is the dashboard.

New studs this step: 12 Light gray, 32 Teal

Step 3: Lower body, second brick layer (lights + seats)

Add a second layer of teal. Press yellow 1x1 headlights into the front row (2nd and 5th stud), red 1x1 tail lights into the back corners, and dark-gray 1x2 seats against each side wall (rows 5-6). The wheel arches now close over the front and rear wheels.

New studs this step: 48 Teal, 2 Yellow, 4 Dark gray, 2 Red

Step 4: Windows, first layer

Build the window band. White bricks are the pillars; clear bricks are the windows: windshield at the front, a door window each side, two camper windows each side, and a rear window. The inside is open.

New studs this step: 16 White, 16 Trans-clear / light blue

Step 5: Windows, second layer (same map as step 4)

Repeat step 4 exactly, so the windows are two bricks tall.

New studs this step: 16 White, 16 Trans-clear / light blue

Step 6: Roof

Cover the van with a white 6x12 plate roof. This part lifts off, so your nephew can play inside.

New studs this step: 72 White

Step 7: Surfboard on the roof

Press a yellow 2x8 plate (or 2x4 + 2x4) on the roof in the middle two columns. That's the surfboard!

New studs this step: 16 Yellow
