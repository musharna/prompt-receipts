# Studies in impermanence

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No installation or network connection is needed.

A silent, 12-second stylized title-sequence motion study, with three four-second shots:

1. A falling teacup fractures into porcelain shards on a textured stone floor. Procedural ballistic trajectories, floor contact, bounce, and settling drive the fragments.
2. Smoke drifts and curls upward from the glowing wick of an already-snuffed candle.
3. A water stream pours into a transparent glass bowl, with droplets, spreading ripples, and a rising water level.

Use the shot buttons, timeline, replay, or keyboard shortcuts (Space and 1–3). Playback stops after the sequence. Reduced-motion preferences disable autoplay.

This is a Canvas 2D procedural animation for timing and art-direction review, not a physically accurate fracture/fluid solver or a rendered video. The titles are editable placeholders. The canvas has a 1280 × 720 backing resolution and scales to the browser window.
