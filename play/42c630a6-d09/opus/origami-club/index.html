Sailboat Beginner · square

  1. Coloured side up, turn the square so it's a diamond. Fold it in half corner to corner, folding the bottom-right half behind, to make a triangle.
  2. Turn the triangle so its long folded edge is at the bottom. Fold a strip about a quarter of the way up along that long edge, front layer only.
  3. Turn over and fold the back layer up the same way. The strips stick out at both sides: that's the hull, and the big triangle is the sail.

Swan Beginner · square

  1. White side up, diamond orientation. Fold in half along the vertical diagonal and unfold.
  2. Fold the two lower edges to the centre crease (a kite).
  3. Turn over. Fold the lower edges to the centre again (a thin kite).
  4. Fold the thin bottom point up to the top point (this will be the neck).
  5. Fold the tip of that point back down a little (the head).
  6. Fold the model in half behind, along the centre line.
  7. Hold the body and pull the neck up and away from the body; pinch the base flat so it stays (outside reverse fold).
  8. Pull the head forward the same way. Done! (These last two shaping moves are done by eye and are not drawn on the crease pattern.)

Waterbomb (balloon) Beginner+ · square

  1. Coloured side up: fold both diagonals (valley) and unfold. Turn over; fold in half both ways (valley) and unfold.
  2. Collapse into a waterbomb base: push the sides in so the square folds into a triangle with the point at the top.
  3. Fold the two bottom corners of the front layer up to the top point (makes a diamond).
  4. Fold the left and right corners of the diamond (front layers) in to the centre line.
  5. Fold the loose top flaps down, then tuck them into the pockets of the triangles you just made.
  6. Turn over and repeat steps 3–5 on the back.
  7. Blow gently into the hole at the bottom to inflate it into a cube-ish balloon.

Jumping frog Beginner+ · 3×5 card (5:8)

  1. Use a 3×5 index card (or paper cut 5:8). White side up. Fold the top-right corner to the left edge and unfold; repeat with the top-left corner — an X crease.
  2. Turn over. Fold the top down through the centre of the X and unfold. Turn back over.
  3. Push the sides in at the horizontal crease and bring the top down: a triangle (waterbomb base) on top of the card.
  4. Fold the triangle's bottom corners (top layer) up to its top point — these are the front legs.
  5. Fold the left and right sides of the card behind to the centre.
  6. Fold the bottom edge up to the base of the triangle.
  7. Fold that flap back down in half — this zig-zag is the spring.
  8. Turn over. Press on the back edge and slide your finger off: it jumps!

Crane Intermediate · square

  1. Coloured side up: fold both diagonals (valley), unfold. Turn over; fold in half both ways, unfold.
  2. Collapse into a preliminary (square) base, open end pointing down.
  3. Fold the lower edges of the front layers to the centre line (kite), fold the top triangle down over them, then unfold these three folds.
  4. Petal fold: lift the bottom corner of the front layer up along the horizontal crease, letting the sides fold in on the kite creases. Flatten.
  5. Turn over and repeat steps 3–4: this is the bird base.
  6. Narrow the two lower flaps: fold their outer edges to the centre, front and back.
  7. Inside-reverse fold the two thin flaps up between the layers: one is the neck, one is the tail.
  8. Inside-reverse fold the tip of the neck to make the head. Fold the wings down and gently spread them.
