Shopping list

  • Glass jar ~14 cm wide, wide mouth
  • Cork or glass lid
  • ~400 ml small gravel
  • Small bag activated charcoal
  • ~700 ml terrarium / peat-free soil
  • Cushion moss + sheet moss
  • 2–3 small ferns (e.g. button fern, mini Boston fern)
  • A few smooth pebbles + 1 feature stone
  • Tiny ceramic deer (~4 cm tall)
  • Ribbon + gift tag

Tips

  • Plant ferns first, towards the back, then press moss around them.
  • Keep the deer in front so it isn't hidden by fronds.
  • Long tweezers and a spoon make placing things through the neck easier.
  • Mist lightly; a closed jar needs very little water.
